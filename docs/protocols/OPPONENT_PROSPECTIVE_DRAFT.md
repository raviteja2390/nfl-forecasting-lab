Do not silently truncate history because data are missing.

At both history levels use eight prior regular-season games, exclude same-season-week outcomes and retain the 48-hour assumed availability delay. Opponent ratings for a historical matchup use that matchup's kickoff minus 24 hours, even if more recent games are available at the target cutoff. Pre-collection historical scores can only be verified as observed by the NEW forecast cutoff; we cannot prove historical publication time before the old matchup. This residual historical-timing assumption is explicitly retained, not replaced by invented receipts. Current observations cannot be backdated.

Valid pairs: identical event ID, kickoff and actual cutoff; approved model/code hashes; both training completions before issuance; both generated at least 24 hours before kickoff; original immutable forecast and source receipt hashes preserved. No predictions from a different version may substitute. Freeze the qualifying reference cohort using the same timing conditions regardless of whether candidate data are available, so missing candidate coverage cannot improve the denominator.

Evaluation: log loss primary; unscaled multiclass Brier, accuracy, tie calibration, exclusions and coverage secondary. One fixed review at 2027-02-08T12:00:00Z, limited to final result receipts observed by then and confirmed by two sources. Withhold disputed outcomes; preserve corrections as append-only receipts. No early promotion or repeated significance testing. Interim outputs are descriptive.

These are practical decision thresholds, not a power guarantee. This is one predeclared candidate comparison; any additional candidates require a new multiplicity plan before issuance. The older player-family protocol remains unchanged.
