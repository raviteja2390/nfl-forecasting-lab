# Opponent-adjusted prospective protocol — approved version 1

Status: APPROVED by the user in this project conversation; approval recorded at 2026-09-26T17:05:39Z. This protocol governs implementation of the opponent-adjusted shadow comparison. Technical activation remains disabled until the issuer and evaluation integration are implemented and verified against this protocol. No forecasts were issued at approval. The evaluation terms below are unchanged from the approved draft.

Candidate: frozen opponent-adjusted-v1; reference: frozen outcome-logit-v1. Their artifact manifest hashes are recorded in the accompanying audit report. No in-place refitting, calibration changes or feature changes are permitted.

Frozen reference identity: throughout this cohort and its final review, the reference remains specifically `outcome-logit-v1` (model-file SHA-256 `c67dd15b7f6c45db1f62d386783f9158b3994675240bb63b83aa10a0a50c34f0`), regardless of whether `outcome-logit-v2` or any other version becomes the operational primary during the cohort window or before review. Continue preserving and issuing eligible `outcome-logit-v1` reference forecasts for this comparison; never substitute the current primary, splice versions, or backfill missing reference predictions. Missing reference evidence must be disclosed and may leave this cohort inconclusive. A comparison against a different reference requires a separately preregistered future cohort and cannot alter this cohort’s reference retrospectively.

Cohort: 2026 regular-season games on Eastern dates from the later of 2026-10-01 or the first Eastern calendar date at least 48 hours after recorded user approval, through 2027-02-01. The exact start date, approval time, model hashes, feature-code hash and this protocol's hash must be committed in a machine-readable activation record BEFORE the first forecast. This is a new comparison, separate from the already registered player-model cohort. Do not backfill games or reuse past candidate outcomes to qualify.

Feature rules: same actual feature cutoff as the reference, at or before kickoff minus 24 hours; issue at or before kickoff minus 24 hours. Only archived source responses observed by the cutoff may contribute. The schedule snapshot and ESPN scoreboards must be no more than 24 hours old at that cutoff. Independently confirm all baseline AND nested opponent history scores final and matching. Refuse absent, stale, mismatched or post-cutoff evidence; report exclusions. Do not silently truncate history because data are missing.

At both history levels use eight prior regular-season games, exclude same-season-week outcomes and retain the 48-hour assumed availability delay. Opponent ratings for a historical matchup use that matchup's kickoff minus 24 hours, even if more recent games are available at the target cutoff. Pre-collection historical scores can only be verified as observed by the NEW forecast cutoff; we cannot prove historical publication time before the old matchup. This residual historical-timing assumption is explicitly retained, not replaced by invented receipts. Current observations cannot be backdated.

Valid pairs: identical event ID, kickoff and actual cutoff; approved model/code hashes; both training completions before issuance; both generated at least 24 hours before kickoff; original immutable forecast and source receipt hashes preserved. No predictions from a different version may substitute. Freeze the qualifying reference cohort using the same timing conditions regardless of whether candidate data are available, so missing candidate coverage cannot improve the denominator.

Evaluation: log loss primary; unscaled multiclass Brier, accuracy, tie calibration, exclusions and coverage secondary. One fixed review at 2027-02-08T12:00:00Z, limited to final result receipts observed by then and confirmed by two sources. Withhold disputed outcomes; preserve corrections as append-only receipts. No early promotion or repeated significance testing. Interim outputs are descriptive.

Eligibility: >=200 paired games across >=12 season-week blocks; >=95% of eligible reference games covered; mean log-loss improvement >=0.010; paired whole season-week bootstrap, 20,000 draws, seed 20260926, two-sided 95% percentile interval with lower bound >0; Brier deterioration <=0.005; no unresolved integrity failures. These are practical decision thresholds, not a power guarantee. This is one predeclared candidate comparison; any additional candidates require a new multiplicity plan before issuance. The older player-family protocol remains unchanged.

Insufficient evidence at review means inconclusive and retain the primary. Passing permits manual review only, not automatic promotion or a profitability claim. Approval arriving too late to meet sample thresholds does not justify extending the window after looking at outcomes; register a new future cohort instead.
