# Exploratory evaluation register

`evaluations/*.json` is the append-only record. Initial entries are explicitly backfilled from checksum-verified saved reports, not retrospectively presented as preregistration. Dates are the reports' original evaluation timestamps. Null CI status means not computed, never a nonsignificant test. The margin model has no bootstrap comparison and remains shelved.

Before evaluating a new candidate, register its design and cohort. After any result is inspected, append a record even if unfavorable; include model/version, parameter or timing variant, cohort, date, reference, signed log-loss improvement, interval or explicit not-computed status, and source report hash. Use `python forecasting/research_register.py --entry /path/to/entry.json`. Existing IDs cannot be overwritten; record corrections in a new linked entry rather than deleting the old record. Keep reports immutable. The register is application-enforced and Git-audited, not admin-proof storage.

Current inventory: 26 comparisons on the 2023 development cohort (20 hyperparameter trials plus 6 timing diagnostics), and 5 on each of 2024, 2025 and the combined 2024–2025 cohort. The latter are the original team model against training-frequency probabilities plus four challengers against the team model. Counting the original comparison is conservative. Selected variants are not counted a second time on 2023. Timing comparisons with identical outputs remain logged. Overlapping cohorts are not independent replications.

`review()` validates source hashes, checks the known historical inventory, detects unregistered saved model versions, counts comparisons, and recomputes the opponent model's Bonferroni sensitivity interval with confidence 1 - 0.05/m. The original unadjusted interval remains preserved. Do not call null intervals negative findings. Do not infer control of all search behavior from a ledger: undocumented work outside this repository cannot be detected. Future evaluators must register every tested variant, including subgroup/metric searches; a test on a different subgroup cannot evade the family simply by renaming its cohort. Define combined multiplicity families in the future protocol when outcomes overlap. Current per-cohort counts are descriptive and do not claim familywise control across subgroup searches.

Historical comparisons NEVER authorize promotion, corrected or otherwise. Fixed-count Bonferroni does not solve indefinite adaptive search or repeated optional stopping. Fresh, preregistered prospective evidence is required. The original two-player protocol already uses 97.5% intervals for two registered comparisons; its cohort, review date and thresholds remain unchanged. Its evaluation now checks register integrity before declaring eligibility. Register failures block integrity, and no automatic promotion exists.

Ensembling is deferred: do not fit blend weights or add candidates while waiting for the current prospective cohort. A future team/player blend is a new candidate with a declared weight-selection method and independent future evaluation.
